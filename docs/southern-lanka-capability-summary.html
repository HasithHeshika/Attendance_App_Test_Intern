The five modules below are listed in dependency order: each one relies on the data and permissions established by the one above it. A department cannot be managed before its company exists; a Head of Department cannot be scoped before departments are assigned; late and early minutes cannot be calculated before a shift is rostered.

Capabilities marked Southern Lanka only are gated by tenant feature flags and do not appear on any other deployment. Those marked Automated run on a schedule without operator action.

01 — FOUNDATION

Tenant & Multi-Company Hierarchy

One deployment serves several organisations. Southern Lanka's data lives in its own isolated database, resolved automatically from the domain, with its own branding and its own set of enabled modules.

  • Domain-resolved data isolation

    The database is selected per request from the hostname — carecode.org resolves to the dedicated southernlanka database. No Southern Lanka record is ever readable from another tenant's domain, and no rebuild or separate deployment is needed.

  • Per-tenant module switches

    Optional modules are turned on or off by feature flag rather than by hard-coded checks. Southern Lanka runs with Payroll and the changelog enabled; the Suspense expense-float module and the Solar cross-link are switched off.

  • Independent brand identity

    Application name, theme colour, favicon, install icons and PWA manifest are all served from the tenant's own asset set, so the installed app presents as CareCode throughout.

  • Company register

    Create and edit companies with address, logo, brand accent colour and an assigned supervisor list. Logo URLs are validated on entry rather than failing silently in the UI.

  • Department tree with sub-departments Southern Lanka only

    Every department belongs to exactly one company and may nest to any depth through a parent department. Departments can be activated, deactivated, and deleted — deletion is a recoverable soft delete, never a permanent erase.

  • Employee-to-hierarchy mapping

    Each employee record carries its company and department. This single mapping drives every downstream scope: who appears on a roster, whose attendance a manager can open, and where a leave request is routed.

  • Cross-tenant administrator access

    Active super administrators are mirrored into every tenant database so they can sign in and administer any organisation, with safeguards that prevent a mirrored account from overwriting a real employee record.

02 — ACCESS CONTROL

User Management & Role-Based Access

Roles are configuration, not code. Administrators create job titles, tick the capabilities each one carries, and place it in the approval hierarchy — all from the application, with no release required.

  • Data-driven role registry

    Roles are stored records with a display name, category, position in the tree and an active flag. Built-in roles are protected from deletion; everything else is fully editable by a System Administrator.

  • Granular capability model

    Thirty-eight independent capability switches gate every page and action — own attendance, approvals, leave administration, schedules, day offs, departments, companies, users, notifications, reports and each payroll stage. Each is described in plain language on the Roles screen.

  • Separate view and manage rights Southern Lanka only

    Departments, Companies and Schedules each carry a read-only capability alongside the manage capability, so a role can be given visibility without edit rights — independently of blanket user administration.

  • Approval hierarchy

    Each role names its parent, forming the approval tree. Ancestors, descendants and peer lines are resolved from that tree to determine who may approve whom, with cycle protection when a role is re-parented.

  • Trainee access variants

    Every employee role carries a second capability set applied automatically when the person's employment type is Trainee — typically attendance and leave without approval or administration rights.

  • System Administrator override

    The System Administrator role implies every management capability, so a newly added capability never has to be granted retroactively and an administrator can never be locked out by an incomplete role registry.

  • Per-user Super Admin override Southern Lanka only

    A single toggle on a user's record grants that individual full System Administrator access everywhere — approvals, pages and reports — without changing their job title or the capabilities of everyone else holding that role.

  • User directory and lifecycle

    Add, view, edit, deactivate and reset access for employees, with full personal, contact, employment and emergency detail, plus validated NIC, phone and email entry. A read-only directory capability exists for roles that need visibility only.

  • Bulk user import

    Employees can be onboarded in bulk from a spreadsheet rather than entered one at a time, with parsing and validation before anything is written.

  • Self-registration with administrator approval Southern Lanka only

    Staff can register themselves on carecode.org. The account is held with a placeholder employee number and flagged as pending until an administrator assigns the real number, which re-keys the record.

  • Attendance method policy per employee Southern Lanka only

    Each employee is explicitly permitted to mark attendance by fingerprint terminal, by mobile application, or both. Records predating the policy are shown as "Not set" rather than silently assumed.

03 — DELEGATED AUTHORITY

HOD Scope & Reporting Line Management

Head of Department is a role; the departments a person heads are assigned to them individually. That assignment is the boundary for everything a HOD can see and approve, and it is enforced when data is loaded, not merely hidden in the interface.

  • Multi-department assignment

    A Head of Department selects any number of departments on their user record. One person can head several departments across the hierarchy, independently of the department they personally sit in.

  • Strict scope resolution

    A HOD role with no departments actually assigned resolves to no access — never to "sees everything". The scope never falls back to the person's own home department, which is where they sit rather than what they manage.

  • Enforcement at the data layer

    Employee lists, attendance records, roster assignments and stored monthly summaries are all filtered to the managed departments as they load, so out-of-scope staff are never held in the session at all.

  • Company pinning and filter locking

    A scoped HOD's company is resolved from their departments and locked, with the company picker hidden. A single-department HOD sees that department pre-selected and disabled; a multi-department HOD gets an "All My Departments" option and their own departments only.

  • Leave approval routing

    A leave request fans out to every active Head of Department covering the applicant's department, plus every approving tier above them in the role tree. A HOD is never included as an approver of their own request — it escalates upward instead.

  • Attendance edit routing

    Attendance correction requests route to the covering Head of Department, falling back to the nearest approving tier when no HOD covers the applicant, rather than broadcasting to every approver.

  • Overtime approval routing Southern Lanka only

    Overtime requests follow the same routing as leave — covering Heads of Department plus the role tree above them — behind a dedicated approval capability.

  • Roster and day-off authority

    A Head of Department with assigned departments automatically receives day-off declaration and management rights, and reaches the Schedule and Attendance View screens without needing blanket user-administration capability.

  • Consistent gating end to end

    Navigation, route access and page content all apply the identical scope test, so a link never appears for a screen the person cannot open.

04 — ROSTERING

Shift Definition & Scheduling Engine

Shifts are defined once and reused across departments. Rostering is a calendar grid of employees against dates, supporting split days, back-to-back duty, shifts that cross midnight, recurring weekly patterns and bulk import from spreadsheet.

  • Reusable shift templates Southern Lanka only

    A shift is a name and a 24-hour time window, offered under any number of departments at once — one "Morning Shift" can serve Nursing and Reception simultaneously. Shifts can be deactivated, and deletion is refused while a live roster still references them.

  • Restricted shift eligibility

    A shift can be limited to named employees and named roles on top of its department scope. A restricted shift saved with no department becomes organisation-wide — the mechanism behind Head of Department and designated executive shifts.

  • Overnight shift handling

    A shift whose end time falls at or before its start is treated as crossing midnight consistently across assignment, overlap detection, hour totals and attendance scoring, and is labelled with a next-day marker so it is never misread as an earlier same-day time.

  • Calendar roster grid

    Select a department to see its employees as rows and every date of the month as columns, with a frozen employee column, month and year jump, and search within the department.

  • Cell assignment and split days

    Clicking a cell offers the shifts that employee is eligible for on that date. More than one shift can be placed on the same cell, covering both back-to-back duty and genuine split days.

  • Overlap warning

    Assigning a shift whose window overlaps one already placed on that cell raises an explicit confirmation naming the clashing shift, so the decision is made deliberately.

  • Holiday classification captured at assignment

    Poya, Public and Mercantile holidays are shown on the date header and stamped onto the assignment when it is made. Editing the holiday list later never retroactively changes what a past roster recorded.

  • Leave and day-off overlays

    Approved and pending leave, and declared day offs, appear as distinct overlays on the grid. A shift can still be assigned over them, with the conflict visible before the decision rather than discovered afterwards.

  • Day-off declaration and bulk import

    Day offs are declared per employee and date, imported in bulk from a spreadsheet, or removed, with the source of each recorded as manual, imported or recurring.

  • Recurring weekly patterns Automated

    A pattern captures "this shift, every selected weekday, from this date" with an optional end date. It writes ordinary roster entries on a rolling horizon, extended weekly by a scheduled job, so every existing view and report reads it unchanged. Recurring day-off patterns work the same way for Heads of Department and designated executives.

  • Occurrence and series control

    A recurring entry can be removed for a single date or the whole series ended, with the number of future dates cleared reported back. The engine only ever touches entries it created — a hand-placed shift on the same date is left alone.

  • Bulk roster import

    A monthly roster spreadsheet — one row per employee, one column per date, shift names separated by comma or slash — is parsed into reviewable draft rows. Names are matched case-insensitively against the department's registered shifts; anything unrecognised is flagged rather than guessed, and nothing is written until the reviewer confirms.

  • Monthly workload totals

    Each employee row carries their assigned shift count, total assigned hours for the month and approved leave days, calculated with correct handling of overnight windows.

  • Employee self-service schedule

    Staff see their own roster on a calendar with a next-shift lookahead, updating live as the roster changes — read-only, and scoped strictly to themselves.

05 — ATTENDANCE

Attendance Tracking & Real-Time Roster Engine

A monthly attendance matrix scored against each employee's actual rostered shifts rather than a fixed clock-in cutoff. Fingerprint and mobile punches land in the same record, and anything implausible is flagged for a supervisor rather than silently corrected.

  • Monthly attendance matrix

    One row per employee and one column per day, with a frozen employee column, month stepping, and filters by name, employee number, EPF number, department and role. Fully scoped for Heads of Department.

  • Fingerprint and mobile capture in one record

    Fingerprint terminal punches are written into the same attendance sessions the mobile application uses, so they appear immediately in every existing report with no separate reconciliation. Each punch shows its own source badge — terminal or mobile — for check-in and check-out independently.

  • Terminal integration safeguards

    The terminal interface is idempotent per scan, debounces duplicate punches, validates the device and employee, and returns coded errors. A rejected scan is not locked in, so an offline terminal's retry queue still succeeds once the underlying issue is fixed.

  • Multi-session and overnight days

    A day holds any number of check-in and check-out cycles. A session that runs past midnight stays filed under the day it started, so an overnight shift is counted once and in the right place.

  • Contiguous duty merging

    Same-day shifts that meet exactly — a zero-minute gap — are merged into one duty block anchored at the first start and the last end, so a missing punch between two back-to-back shifts is never counted as a late arrival or an early departure.

  • Split-day separation

    Same-day shifts with a real gap between them stay separate blocks, each matched to its own attendance session in order and scored on its own. The gap is never counted as working time.

  • Shortfall calculation against the roster

    Late minutes are measured from the block's scheduled start to the actual check-in; early departure from the actual check-out to the block's scheduled end. Both floor at zero, so arriving early or staying late never offsets a shortfall elsewhere.

  • At-a-glance cell states

    Every cell reads as on-time, late, early departure, absent, future or holiday, with additional markers distinguishing merged continuous duty from split shifts on the same day.

  • Cell detail view

    Opening a cell shows each duty block's scheduled window against the actual punches, the constituent shifts of a merged block, the capture method for each punch, the holiday classification, and the terminal device identifier retrieved on demand.

  • Inline quick-assign

    A missing roster entry can be assigned retrospectively from inside the cell itself, filtered to shifts that employee is eligible for. Cell colour, late and early figures and the monthly totals all recalculate immediately, and the panel stays open so a split day can receive its second shift.

  • Persisted monthly totals

    Total late minutes, total early-departure minutes, late days and early-departure days are stored per employee per month as a stable figure for HR to act on. The screen reports; it never deducts pay or leave on its own.

  • Exception review workflow

    Implausibly long sessions, sessions reopened after a missed check-out, and sessions split by a back-dated roster change raise a review item that moves from flagged, to in review, to resolved — recorded as a time correction, an approved overtime case, or accepted as is, with a note and optional link to the related request.

  • Raw punches are never rewritten

    Original timestamps are preserved exactly as captured. Interpretation lives in a separate layer that allocates a session into rostered and unverified-extra time, so the audit trail of what the terminal actually recorded stays intact.

  • Open-session monitor Automated

    A scheduled job runs every fifteen minutes and flags sessions still open past the plausible maximum. It only ever raises a review — it never closes a session or truncates recorded hours.

  • Recalculation on retrospective roster change

    Back-dating, editing or removing a roster entry queues a recalculation for that employee and date range, so attendance interpretation stays consistent with the roster it is scored against.

  • Attendance correction requests

    Employees raise correction requests against a record; approvers consider, approve or reject them with a reason, and the outcome is recorded against the request.

Scope and exclusions

  • Payroll is excluded by request. It is implemented for Southern Lanka — payroll settings, employee pay profiles, monthly runs with separate generate, review and finalise authority, loans, salary advances, payslips and payroll reports — and is summarised separately.
  • Leave management is referenced here only where it touches HOD routing and the roster overlays. Leave types, balances, accrual rules and the request lifecycle are a module in their own right.
  • The Suspense expense-float module is built but switched off for Southern Lanka, and is not part of this register.
  • Working patterns — configurable expected hours and rest days per role, location or company — are designed but not yet built, and are tracked as planned work.
